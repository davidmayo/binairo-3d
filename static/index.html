THE RULES

Every slice must work.

01

Balance the colors

Every line contains exactly two coral and two blue circles.

02

No triples

Never place more than two circles of the same color next to each other.

03

Keep lines unique

No two rows or columns in any 2D slice may be identical.

HOW TO PLAY

One cube. Twelve puzzles.

Click an empty circle to make it coral, click again for blue, and once more to clear it. Use the layer controls to move through the cube.

Each horizontal, vertical, and depth-facing 4×4 slice must be a valid Binairo grid. Conflicting circles are marked as soon as a rule is broken.
